Last Mile Connectivity
Version 1 — Dynamic Last Mile Assignment Model

1

Problem Description

Version 0 considered a static problem in which all passenger requests and vehicle positions were
known beforehand and a single optimization was performed. Version 1 generalises this to a dynamic
setting in which passenger requests arrive continuously over time and vehicles are in motion while
decisions are being made.
The system operates in discrete time stamps of fixed length ∆t (for example, 30 seconds). At every
time stamp, the Version-0 static problem is solved on the passengers and vehicles currently in the
system. Passengers who are not assigned in a given time stamp are carried over to the next time
stamp, with their remaining waiting-time tolerance reduced by the elapsed interval. The Version0 objective, constraints, decision variables, and greedy algorithm are reused without modification
within each time stamp.
Scenario Description
• Passenger requests arrive continuously over time rather than all at once.
• Vehicles are in motion between time stamps; their positions evolve at a known speed.
• Each time stamp is treated as a Version-0 static sub-problem on the current pool.
• Unassigned passengers are not lost; they are carried over until either assigned or timed out.

Sets

The Version-0 sets are retained. A new set is added for time stamps:
• Passengers: P = {1, 2, . . . , p}
• Vehicles: V = {1, 2, . . . , v}
• Stops: S = {1, 2, . . . , s}
• Soft routes: R = {1, 2, . . . , r}
• Time stamps: T = {0, ∆t, 2∆t, . . .}

Termination. In a real deployment, the loop runs indefinitely. For experimental evaluation, it
terminates after a fixed simulation horizon Tsim .
Remark. Version 1 reuses the entire Version-0 model as an inner solver. The only genuinely new
ingredients are: (i) a discrete time stamp set T , (ii) the carry-over rule with the decrementing
Wp , and (iii) vehicles physically advancing at speed νv between time stamps. All other elements—
objective, constraints, greedy algorithm, cost calculation—are identical to Version 0.
